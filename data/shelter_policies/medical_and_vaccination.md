# Medical and Vaccination Policy

## Vaccinations before adoption
Dogs receive DHPP and, if older than 3 months, rabies vaccination. Cats receive
FVRCP and, if older than 3 months, rabies vaccination. Puppies and kittens often
need booster shots after adoption, and the adopter is responsible for completing them.

## Spay and neuter
All animals are spayed or neutered before going home, unless a veterinarian decides
the animal is too young or has a medical reason to wait. In that case, the adopter
signs an agreement to complete the surgery by 6 months of age, and the shelter
covers the cost at a partner clinic.

## Microchips
All dogs and cats are microchipped before adoption. The microchip registration is
transferred to the adopter's name.

## Animals with ongoing medical needs
All known medical conditions are shared with the adopter before adoption. The
shelter does not give medical advice. Please consult a licensed veterinarian about
diagnosis or treatment. Ongoing medical costs after adoption are the adopter's
responsibility.

## Medical records and first vet visit
Adopters receive copies of all medical records at adoption. Each adoption includes
one free wellness exam at a partner clinic if it is used within 14 days.
