# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I adopt if I work full time
Yes. Many of our adopters work full time. During the interview we will talk
about your schedule and help you choose an animal whose needs fit your routine.

## Can I adopt an animal as a gift
We do not allow surprise gift adoptions. The person who will care for the animal
must apply and attend the meet-and-greet.

## When is the shelter open
The shelter is open to visitors on Saturdays and Sundays from 10 am to 4 pm.
Weekday visits are by appointment only.

## Can I volunteer or foster
Yes. We hold a volunteer orientation on the first Saturday of every month. Fostering
requires a separate foster application.

## Can I adopt more than one animal
Yes. Adopting two animals at once is welcome, especially bonded pairs. Each animal
requires its own meet-and-greet.
