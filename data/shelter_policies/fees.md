# Adoption Fees

## Fee schedule
- Puppies (under 1 year): $300
- Adult dogs (1 to 7 years): $250
- Senior dogs (over 7 years): $100
- Cats and kittens: $150
- Bonded pair of cats: $200 for both
- Rabbits and other small animals: $75

## What the fee covers
The adoption fee covers vaccinations given so far, spay or neuter surgery,
a microchip, deworming, flea treatment, and a veterinary exam. The fee also helps
pay for the care of animals still waiting for homes.

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, Zelle, and personal checks. We do not accept
cash. The fee is paid when you sign the adoption agreement.

## Discounts and fee waivers
Veterans and adopters aged 65 or older receive 50% off the fee for adult and
senior animals. We occasionally hold fee-waived adoption events, which are
announced on our social media pages. Fees for puppies are never waived.

## Are fees refundable
Adoption fees are non-refundable, except during the trial period described in the
Trial Period and Returns policy.
