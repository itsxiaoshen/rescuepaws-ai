# Transport and Pickup

## Picking up at the shelter
Animals living at the main shelter can be picked up on Saturdays and Sundays
between 10 am and 4 pm. Please bring a leash and collar for dogs or a secure
carrier for cats and small animals.

## Picking up from a foster home
Some animals live with foster volunteers. Pickup from a foster home is arranged
directly with the foster coordinator after the adoption agreement is signed.

## Delivery by volunteer drivers
Volunteer drivers can deliver an adopted animal to homes within 100 miles of the
shelter. We ask for a $50 contribution toward gas. We do not deliver further than
100 miles.

## The first ride home
Use a crate or carrier for the first ride home. Go straight home without stopping
at parks or stores. Keep car windows mostly closed, and never leave the animal
alone in the car.
