# Adoption Process

## Steps to adopt
1. Browse available animals and pick one or two you would like to meet.
2. Submit an online adoption application.
3. Complete a short phone interview with an adoption volunteer.
4. Attend a meet-and-greet with the animal.
5. Complete a home check (dog adoptions only).
6. Sign the adoption agreement and pay the adoption fee.
7. Take your new companion home.

## Application form
The application is online and takes about 15 minutes. You will need a valid
government ID. If you rent, include your landlord's contact information.
Applications are reviewed within 3 business days, and a volunteer will contact
you by phone or email.

## Meet-and-greet
A meet-and-greet is required for every adoption. It takes 30 to 60 minutes and
happens at the shelter or at the animal's foster home. Every member of the
household, including children, should attend. If you are adopting a dog and
already have a dog, your dog must attend a supervised dog-to-dog introduction.

## Home check
A home check is required for dog adoptions only. A volunteer visits in person or
by video call to look at fencing, escape risks, and household hazards. Cat and
small-animal adoptions do not require a home check.

## How long adoption takes
Most adoptions take 1 to 2 weeks from application to take-home day. Timing
depends on volunteer availability and how quickly references and landlords respond.

## Placing a hold on an animal
After your application is approved, you can place a hold on one animal for up to
48 hours while you arrange the meet-and-greet. Only one hold is allowed at a time.
