# Adopter Eligibility

## Basic requirements
Adopters must be at least 21 years old, show a valid government ID, and live
within 100 miles of the shelter.

## Renters
Renters must provide their landlord's contact information or a copy of a lease
that allows pets. We follow any breed or size restrictions in your lease.

## Households with children
Families with children are welcome to adopt. For homes with children under 5,
we recommend animals whose records show they have been observed safely with
children. All children living in the home should attend the meet-and-greet.

## Households with other pets
Pets already in your home should be spayed or neutered and up to date on
vaccinations. Dog adoptions into a home with another dog require a supervised
dog-to-dog introduction before approval.

## Reasons an application may be declined
An application may be declined for a history of animal neglect or cruelty, false
information on the application, or a landlord who does not allow pets. Decisions
are made by the adoption committee. Applicants may reapply after 6 months.
