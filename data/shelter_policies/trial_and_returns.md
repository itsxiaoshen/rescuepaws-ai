# Trial Period and Returns

## Trial period
Every adoption includes a 14-day trial period. The adoption fee is paid at the
start. If the animal is returned within 14 days, we refund the fee minus a $25
administrative charge.

## Returns after the trial period
We will always take back an animal we placed, at any time in its life, for any
reason. Fees are not refunded after the 14-day trial period.

## How to return an animal
Email adoptions@rescuepaws.example to schedule a return appointment. Please bring
any veterinary records from after the adoption. We aim to schedule returns within
7 days of your request.

## Rehoming an adopted animal
The adoption agreement requires you to contact the shelter before rehoming. You may
not sell an adopted animal or give it to another person without our approval.
